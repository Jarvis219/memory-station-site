● TODAY’S TRAIN ON TIME
Platform · About

Save small moments as memory tickets — a few lines, a mood, photos from your library or straight from the camera, or a voice note. Swipe through each memory’s photo album, look back on this day in years past (by the solar or lunar calendar), write letters to your future self, and see any year as a train journey.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no servers, no tracking. The app works offline; the only optional connection is a new-version check you can turn on yourself.
